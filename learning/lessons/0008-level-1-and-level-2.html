Where Levels 1 and 2 show up

The evidence for the supporting levels, and the question "where's Level 2?"

After this lessonYou can point to concrete Level 1 and Level 2 evidence in the demo.

What the levels require

Level 1: specialized agents with distinct capabilities collaborate on a task. Level 2: agents find collaborators by capability, respond to at least one unexpected failure or change, and explain why each was chosen. We claim Level 3 and show these two as supporting evidence.

Source: the Cisco challenge statement.

Level 1 in HarvestNet

Inside each organization, specialists feed the negotiator. A food bank has demand, storage, hours, truck, and distribution specialists. A donor has inventory, condition, deadline, and drop-off specialists. The negotiator has to weigh findings that conflict. For example, its storage specialist reports space for 200 lb, but its distribution specialist reports that only 120 lb can go out before it spoils.

Level 2 in HarvestNet

The city food bank's cooler fails. Its agent:

  • searches the AGNTCY Directory for food banks with cold space
  • asks each candidate about its availability
  • writes a reason card for every candidate, including the ones it rejects

Example reason card: "Rural food bank: has cold space, but pickup ends at 6 pm, so its households can't collect romaine tonight. Not chosen."

The produce profile decides what has to move: romaine needs cold, but apples and potatoes can stay where they are.

The hard question

"Where's your Level 2?"

When a food bank's cooler fails, its agent finds a replacement by capability through the Directory, explains every candidate with a reason card, and re-runs approvals. Only the items that actually need cold get moved.
