The 3-minute demo

Seven moments, what to say at each, and which level each one proves.

After this lessonYou can narrate the demo and tie each moment to what it proves.

The seven moments

  • 1. The household call (voice, in Spanish). A family of four, near Watsonville, can pick up after 6 pm, has a small fridge, and receives CalFresh. They hear their fair share (12 lb) and choose food banks. Proves: real people can use it with no app.
  • 2. The farmers market reports leftovers. 300 lb of apples, 200 lb of romaine, 400 lb of potatoes. Proves: the SNAP produce-program link, and that any donor can join.
  • 3. The agents negotiate. Food banks bid item by item. The advocate argues for the rural food bank, whose households are furthest behind. The donor and the food banks agree who moves what. The draft changes on screen, with reasons. Proves: Level 3.
  • 4. A live approval. A manager approves through n8n. Proves: humans approve their own commitments.
  • 5. The cooler fails. Flip the Beeceptor switch. The Directory finds the eastside food bank, with reason cards, and only the romaine moves. Proves: Level 2, plus the produce profile at work.
  • 6. The parent gets a text. 8 lb of apples and potatoes at the rural food bank, 6 to 8 pm, with a code, plus a Market Match tip. Proves: the fair share in action.
  • 7. The results strip. Pounds wasted, fair-share coverage, and households reached, compared with first-come-first-served. Proves: evidence, reported honestly.

Tips

  • Keep a recorded replay as a backup, and label it as a replay if you use it.
  • If negotiation runs long, show the version history instead of waiting.
  • Close with the one-liner from Lesson 1.
