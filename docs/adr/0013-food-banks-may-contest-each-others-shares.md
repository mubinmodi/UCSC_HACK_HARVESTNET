# Food banks may contest each other's shares

October 27, 2026, decided by the lead after an adversarial review found that food banks never really disagreed. Each food bank could change only its own line, so whoever bid first kept the produce, and the advocate passed every turn because nothing above a gap was left to move. The review called it "an ordered greedy fill with a negotiation log." This narrows how food banks bid; it does not change ADR 0001.

## Decision

- **A food bank may contest another food bank's share.** While its own households are below fair share and no unclaimed pounds of an item it can receive remain, its counter may move pounds of that item from another food bank's line to its own, citing its own fair-share gap. It sees only its own facts and the shared draft, never the other food bank's gap.
- **The food bank that loses pounds answers for itself.** On its turn it concedes pounds it holds beyond its own fair-share gap, and it may take back pounds that are within its gap, citing its own gap. It never fights for pounds beyond fair share.
- **The advocate takes a side but has no vote.** It sees every gap, so it may counter in either direction, toward whichever food bank is further below fair share, and say why.
- **A standoff is kept, not broken.** When contest and take-back repeat the same terms, the lot ends as a preserved disagreement. The dashboards of the food banks involved show both versions and both reasons, and their staff approvers decide.

## Consequences

- ADR 0001 still holds. A contest is a proposal: no pounds leave a food bank's share in any commitment unless that food bank's own staff approver approves the version.
- The rule check that rejected any food bank counter touching another food bank's line now allows exactly one shape: a shift of one item from one other food bank to the proposer. Setting another food bank's pounds, or shifting between two other food banks, is still refused.
- Turn order no longer decides the split on its own, so the comparison against first-come-first-served measures negotiation, not who moved first.
- Results must be re-measured after this change. Earlier evaluation numbers describe the old mechanism.
