# Freeze the framework; evaluate decisions from current evidence

October 10, 2026. The user's latest direction is to use general frameworks, live APIs, guardrails, a harness, and evaluations. Earlier discussion chose increasingly specific allocations, fund amounts, urgency bands, and a dairy-refusal ending. Those examples helped expose constraints, but freezing them as product behavior would make the system reproduce a story rather than respond to available evidence.

## Decision

Freeze the v1 framework: organization authority, data and food-profile contracts, execution-harness responsibilities, guardrails, and the evidence required for evaluations. Organization policies, food profiles, observations, scenario inputs, and resulting allocations remain separately versioned and changeable. Live APIs inform decisions; they cannot authorize spending, establish consent, or replace missing lot evidence.

Use the same assessment and validation path for live shadow runs, recorded replay, and labeled synthetic tests. Shared infrastructure and an evaluation runner never choose for live organizations. Retain the old numerical fixture for accounting regression and sensitivity review, with no prescribed live winner or ending.

## Consequences

- Round 10's detailed fund rule is a candidate organization policy, with its mechanical qualifications documented, rather than a universal product algorithm.
- Cross-event resource competition and preserved disagreement remain required capabilities to evaluate. The exact food split, grant amount, and refused event follow the run.
- Demonstrations identify current, cached, and synthetic evidence and report results honestly, including losses against the baselines.
- A framework freeze does not certify integration, food eligibility, operational readiness, or measured benefit. Unsupported assessments and missing consequential evidence prevent affected commitments.
- Framework changes need a stated reason and renewed consistency review. Policies, profiles, adapters, and tests record their own versions and evidence without reopening the framework for every new API value.
