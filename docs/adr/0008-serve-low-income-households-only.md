# Serve low-income households only

October 10, 2026, decided by the user: "cut out homeless people, just low income house." This narrows ADR 0006 and ADR 0007. HarvestNet serves **low-income households** with surplus raw fresh produce, through food pantries and community fridges. Serving people experiencing homelessness, and shelters as distribution sites, is deferred.

## Consequences

- Distribution sites in the MVP are food pantries and community fridges.
- The matching rule no longer centers on kitchen access, since households are assumed able to cook. It uses each household's pickup windows, its fridge space, and how long each item lasts, so produce reaches only households that can collect and store it before it spoils.
- The advocate argues for the sites and areas with the lowest coverage, using USDA Food Access Research Atlas context for low-access areas.
- HarvestNet does not ask about income or verify eligibility. Sites keep their own existing rules.
- The headline counts households actually reached, by pickup confirmations.
