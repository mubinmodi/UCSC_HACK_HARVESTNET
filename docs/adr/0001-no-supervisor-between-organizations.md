# No supervisor between organizations

HarvestNet targets Level 3 of the challenge: independent organizations negotiate with no central decision-maker. Each organization may use a supervisor-style workflow *inside* itself, but nothing sits above the organizations. Shared infrastructure only orders messages, records events, and checks rules; it never picks an allocation or accepts on anyone's behalf. The coordinator observes and contacts people, and decides nothing for any organization. This deliberately differs from coffeeAGNTCY's Lungo, where a supervisor directs the other agents. Adding a supervisor between organizations would make the system easier to build but would void the Level 3 claim.

## Consequences

- When negotiation stalls or organizations disagree, the result is a preserved disagreement shown to the coordinator, not a forced pick.
- The rescue fund can attach published conditions to its own money, but it cannot assign food or accept for others.
- Nothing is ever committed automatically, not even when a deadline arrives. With no commitment, the food stays unharvested or unclaimed, and the reasons are recorded.
